**SUMA**

	" + " Suma de Numeros


**RESTA**

	 " - " Resta de Numeros 


**MULTIPLICACION**

	" * " Multiplicacion de numeros


**DIVISION**

	 " / " Division de Numeros


**MODULO**

	 " % " Modulo de numeros


**EJEMPLO** 

```SQL
SELECT price + 10 as increased_price,

    price - 10 as reduced_price,

    price * 10 as multiple_price,

    price / 2 as half_price

FROM products;
```
